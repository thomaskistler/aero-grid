Channel output

1x2 2x1 2x2

Displays the radio's final channel outputs, after output limits and reversal. No aircraft telemetry is required.

Settings

Setting Default What it does
Label CHANNEL OUTPUT Panel heading. Clear it to restore the default.
First channel 1 First channel to show, from 1 to 32. Steps of 1.
Channel count Auto Number of consecutive channels to show, from 1 to 32. Step below 1 to return to Auto, which fills the available rows.
Show channel names on Show up to three letters of the channel name when space permits.
Show trim on Show the corresponding effective stick-trim arrow when it can be identified.
Accent cyan Color used for the normal state and output fill: cyan, green, amber, or orange.

Behavior

The panel can be added to any free grid cell, starting at 1x1, and resized to any span that fits the grid.

Channels appear consecutively starting at First channel. With Channel count on Auto, the panel fills the available rows; a chosen count is capped by the available space and CH32. The panel adjusts when resized. Wide panels show signed output percentages beside the tapes; narrow panels keep the tapes.

Show channel names and Show trim are independent. Hiding names leaves just the channel number and more room for the tape. Hiding trims removes the green arrows without changing output readings.

Channel names come from the model's Outputs page. When an output is unnamed, the panel uses its enabled mix name, Input name, input-line name, or source name, in that order. Conflicting names from active mixes are omitted rather than guessed. Up to three letters follow the channel number, such as CH1: AIL, and disappear when they will not fit. Side-by-side rows omit names unless at least 64 pixels remain for the tape.

A green downward arrow shows the corresponding effective stick trim, not output subtrim or the trim's calculated contribution to servo travel. Its scale starts at standard trim travel and widens to extended travel once an extended value is observed. An ambiguous or unavailable trim has no arrow.

States

Condition Presentation
At least one visible output is readable Output tapes and normal accent. Percentages appear when space permits.
An output is unreadable Its value is -- where values are shown; its fill and trim arrow are hidden.
No visible output is readable N/A badge.
