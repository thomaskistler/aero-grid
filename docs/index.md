# AeroGrid

AeroGrid is a customizable dashboard for EdgeTX color-screen radios.
Choose flight, telemetry, battery, navigation, and control panels, then
arrange them on a 4 x 4 grid with the on-radio editor.

Use **Documentation version** in the header to match your installed release.
**main (unreleased)** describes the development version.

## Why AeroGrid?

Keep the information that matters to your model together, with consistent
readings, colors, and alerts. Save multiple layouts and choose a dark or light
theme to suit your radio.
